Types of Networks: LAN, MAN and WAN

Networks are grouped by the area they cover. A Local Area Network, or LAN, connects devices inside a single building, such as a school computer lab or an office floor. It is fast, cheap to build, and privately owned by the organisation that uses it.

A Metropolitan Area Network, or MAN, spans a city, for example connecting several branches of a bank. A Wide Area Network, or WAN, covers countries and continents, and the internet is the largest WAN in the world. As the coverage area grows, the network becomes more expensive to run and data usually travels at lower speeds.
