What is a Computer Network?

A computer network is a group of two or more computers connected together so they can share information and resources. The connection can be made with cables, such as ethernet, or wirelessly, such as Wi-Fi. Every device on a network is called a node, and the computers people use directly, like desktops and laptops, are called hosts or workstations.

Networks exist so that data can travel quickly between machines. When you open a website, send an email, or print to a shared printer, your computer is taking part in networking. The hardware that makes this possible includes network interface cards, switches, routers, and the transmission media between them, while software such as network protocols sets the rules for how the data moves.
